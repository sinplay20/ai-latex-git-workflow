\section{Internal Working Notes}
\label{app:internal-notes}

This appendix appears only in `paper-working.tex` and is excluded from
`paper-submit.tex`.

Use it for material that must remain versioned but is not part of the
submission, such as:

\begin{itemize}
  \item deferred proof alternatives;
  \item advisor questions;
  \item verification checklists;
  \item longer calculations awaiting editorial decisions.
\end{itemize}
